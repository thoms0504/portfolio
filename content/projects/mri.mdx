MRI is a web survey that measures three dimensions of an employee's profile (motivation, relations and impact) and turns the answers into a readable report.

Main features: a 12-minute countdown survey paged 20 questions at a time, scoring per aspect with top-10 and bottom-5 questions, an AI-written summary per respondent (xAI Grok) with a local fallback when the API is busy, automatic biodata sync from the Wawai API, and an admin panel with Plotly analytics (radar, trends, per-unit and per-employee drill-down), master-employee management and PDF export. Data is stored as CSV files, so no database is needed.
