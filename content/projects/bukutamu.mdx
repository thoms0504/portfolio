This system replaces paper queue tickets and handwritten guest books at BPS service counters (PST) across Lampung, with a separate workspace for each office.

Main features: QR-based visitor check-in from a phone, a live ticket page, automatic Indonesian voice announcements when a number is called, a TV kiosk mode for the waiting room, ping and ready signals between receptionist and officer, multi-officer collaboration on one ticket, service categories with a daily ticket reset, post-service star ratings per officer, Excel duty rosters, attendance, officer statistics with recommendations, Excel and PDF export, employee sync from the Wawai API, and five roles from visitor to superadmin.
