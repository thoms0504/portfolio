SEHAT helps people choose a hospital or doctor by analysing what patients actually write in reviews, instead of relying only on a single star rating.

Main features: reviews scraped from Alodokter and HonestDoc, aspect-based sentiment analysis (IndoBERT and Sastrawi pipelines) across six aspects such as communication, punctuality, cost transparency, facilities, cleanliness and waiting time, a Plotly analytics dashboard with radar charts, doctor profile pages with schedules, and a Gemini chatbot with separate hospital and doctor recommendation modes that can also analyse requirements and symptoms and keeps its own chat history.
