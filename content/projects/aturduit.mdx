AturDuitLoe records income, expenses and investments and turns them into insights, so users can see their financial health at a glance.

Main features: Supabase Row Level Security so every user only ever reads their own rows, 26 default categories plus custom ones, daily to yearly charts, an AI insight (Gemini) with a 0-100 health score, savings rate and three-month trend, CSV export, and an investment portfolio tracker, email login protected by a middleware auth guard, and a dark/light theme with a mobile-friendly layout.
