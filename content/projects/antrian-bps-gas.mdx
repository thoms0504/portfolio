A lightweight edition of the PST queue system that needs no server, database or build step: five Apps Script files deployed as a single web app with Google Sheets as the database.

Main features: QR self-registration with a live ticket page, automatic Indonesian voice calling on the kiosk screen, star ratings from three channels, ping and ready notifications between receptionist and officer, multi-officer collaboration per ticket, Excel roster import and export, and employee sync from the Wawai API.

The kiosk display (see Documentation) is built for the waiting-room screen: a large QR code invites visitors to scan and take a queue number from their own phone, next to a live panel showing the officers currently on duty, the waiting queue and the tickets already served. A clock, the date and a system-status footer sit on the screen, and everything refreshes automatically every 5 seconds without reloading the page.
