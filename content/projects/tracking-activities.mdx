This tool makes daily work activities easy to record and to summarise for reporting.

Main features: create, edit and delete activities with category and status, photo evidence uploaded or taken from the device camera, a dashboard with status counts, pie, monthly bar, heatmap and cumulative progress charts, and CSV export for a chosen date range.
