Riau's peatlands are among Indonesia's most fire-prone areas. This project uses eleven years of satellite data (2015-2025, 132 months) to estimate fire risk before fires start rather than after.

Main features: a monthly dataset from MODIS (burned area, land surface temperature, NDVI), CHIRPS rainfall and ERA5 wind exported through Google Earth Engine, a Fire Risk Index and a 0-4 fire-trigger score (drought, extreme heat, dry vegetation, strong wind), a comparison of several classifiers (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Extra Trees, SVM, Logistic Regression) with time-series cross-validation, burned-area estimation with a Random Forest regressor and an optional Bidirectional LSTM, feature importance, monthly 2026 estimates, and a six-menu Streamlit dashboard with hotspot maps focused on the seven highest-risk districts and a data-quality audit.
