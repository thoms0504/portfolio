DokuKegiatan keeps activity reports, photos and supporting documents organised in the user's own Google Drive as Year, Month, Activity folder, without any server.

Main features: automatic folder structure, a rich-text activity report saved as a PDF plus a Google Doc summary per activity, 0-100% progress with history, in-app camera photos stamped with date, time and activity name, supporting-document uploads, a Chart.js dashboard, and safe deletion to the Drive trash.
