This dashboard lets managers and the HR team explore the results of the 2025 employee assessment without opening spreadsheets, filtering by level and by region, and see which managerial and technical competencies are suboptimal.

Main features: summary cards for the number of records and average managerial and technical scores, status distribution (optimal, fairly optimal, less optimal) per competency, priority-improvement tables with employee counts and average gaps, separate managerial and technical views, an employee mapping page, a summary page with a per-region matrix and filterable employee details, CSV download, and a login-protected admin role.
