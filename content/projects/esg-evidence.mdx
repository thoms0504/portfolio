Assessing a bank's ESG performance normally means reading hundreds of pages of sustainability report by hand. This app finds the relevant evidence for each checklist item automatically.

Main features: page-aware PDF chunking with company and year metadata, multilingual embeddings stored in ChromaDB, two-stage hybrid retrieval (semantic, lexical and justification scoring with a reranker), a 44-item bilingual checklist, rule-based or LLM evidence synthesis (Gemini or OpenRouter), a full-text scan to catch missed evidence, a regression check against golden sets, live progress streaming, document management per company and year, and Excel/Word export for review.
